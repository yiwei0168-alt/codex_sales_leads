# v1.4 independent candidate-value evaluation

## Executive conclusion

The v1.3.1 ranking understated Gemini Full because it treated a provider-neutral extraction failure as if it disproved the company, its networking business or its submitted role. v1.4 separates those concepts. Candidate truth is independently adjudicated; provider citation completeness is displayed separately with zero score weight.

Under the corrected objective—value of the final candidate information to the user—Gemini Full ranks first at **64.93**. Its advantage is concentrated in tier-1 distribution (**91.1**) and remains material in B2B resale (**58.1**); project services is weaker (**45.6**). Google Places Local remains the best tested local project-services route at **70.3** for that lane and ranks second overall at **33.80**.

This is the final v1.4 result under the user-approved correction. No additional blind review was performed because the human-confirmed role and scoring rubric did not change.

## Why the previous Gemini Full result was not valid as the final comparison

Three Gemini figures now have different and explicit meanings:

|---|---:|---|

| v1.4 independent candidate value | **64.93** | Independently verified candidate/lane truth plus the confirmed fit/path rubric. This is the main comparable result. |

The correction does not restore the rejected 78.50. It independently verifies each submitted company and produces a materially lower, auditable score.

## Confirmed v1.4 protocol

Provider output is a candidate proposal. A missing citation or weak model explanation does not itself make a company false. Independent evaluation uses three states:

- `verified-pass`: the company, Germany presence, active-networking relevance and submitted-lane activity are independently supported;
- `verified-fail`: independent review shows at least one substantive value gate does not pass;
- `unresolved`: available information cannot decide the claim; retrieval failure remains unknown and cannot be converted to a negative fact.

Only `verified-fail` can force a specially adjudicated result to zero. All 30 Gemini Full candidates were resolved, so the leaderboard has no unresolved interval.

Decisions are keyed by canonical company and submitted lane, not provider. The 30 Gemini decisions consequently updated 43 occurrences across systems, including shared Bechtle, CANCOM, SVA, netgo, Axians and other candidates.

## Scoring

The substantive value gates are:

1. company exists;
2. Germany presence;
3. active-networking business relevant to the task;
4. genuine activity in the submitted lane;
5. unique canonical company.

Eligible results receive:

`product/use-case fit × 9 + cooperation path × 7 + independent information confidence × 4`

Product/use-case fit and cooperation path provide 80 of 100 points. Independent information confidence provides 20. `provider_evidence_completeness` provides **zero** points and is not an eligibility gate.

For all systems, the old v1.3 `sufficientEvidence` field is removed as a main value gate. A non-adjudicated candidate whose substantive gates are already demonstrated may therefore receive a score with a low confidence level even when the old completeness floor was not met. This avoids a Gemini-only exception.

The tier-1 result is strong because all ten candidates are genuine downstream distributors. Several are especially close to Cudy's product and channel path: [ALLNET](https://distribution.allnet.de/) explicitly supplies resellers and system houses with networking products; [KOMSA](https://komsa.com/b2b-handel) lists routers, switches and access points plus channel support; [Littlebit](https://www.littlebit.de/ubiquiti-netzwerkloesungen) is an official Ubiquiti distributor; and [ECOM](https://www.ecom-trading.de/shop/ubiquiti/) operates a live Ubiquiti networking wholesale catalog.

The B2B result includes eight valid VAR/reseller paths. Examples include [Bechtle's live company-network shop](https://www.bechtle.com/shop/hardware/it-infrastruktur/netzwerk--10006005--c), [CANCOM's hardware-through-implementation network offer](https://www.cancom.de/loesungen/it-themen/network-connectivity/), and [netgo's hardware, LAN/WLAN, installation and operating model](https://www.netgo.de/network/network-as-a-service). SoftwareOne and Skaylink fail because the reviewed public offers do not demonstrate the submitted Cudy-relevant active-network hardware-resale path.

The project-services result is mixed. [SPIE](https://spie.de/leistungen/it-solutions/network-infrastructure) publicly covers selection, supply, installation and lifecycle service for switching, routing, WLAN and LAN; [DATAGROUP](https://www.datagroup.de/en/corbox/network-services/managed-lan-wlan) plans, provides, installs and operates routers, switches and access points. BFE, EQOS Energie, KUMAVISION and GISA are real service companies, but the reviewed evidence does not demonstrate a Cudy-relevant active-network product role in the submitted lane.

The diagnostic is intentionally structural: a row is complete when the provider supplied a non-empty URL and excerpt. It does not say the excerpt proves the claim. The 100% values for most web providers and 0% for Places Local demonstrate why this metric must not drive the value ranking.

## Interpretation and product implication

Gemini Full is the best tested route for quickly producing a high-value, category-balanced shortlist of established channel companies in this German sample. Google Places Local is complementary rather than inferior: it reaches small regional project-service companies that the broad model-led search misses. Product Gemini benefits substantially when shared decisions are applied, but its project-services discovery remains the main weakness.

The product should therefore separate discovery from proof:

- use model-led discovery for structured tier-1 and VAR shortlists;
- use localized Places for small installers and regional service tails;
- resolve company identity and role independently;
- score candidate fit and cooperation value from shared company/lane facts;
- show provider evidence completeness only as a user-interface diagnostic.

## Limitations

- The run covers Germany and three channels only.
- The 30 Gemini Full rows received explicit independent semantic review; other rows retain v1.3.1 shared-evidence evaluation except for the system-neutral removal of the old sufficient-evidence value gate.
- v1.4 measures candidate value after independent verification, not raw provider output cost or latency.
- No new blind audit was added by design. The completed v1.3 human calibration remains the basis for role and score interpretation.
- Public information can change. The artifact records the reviewed URLs and should be refreshed before operational outreach.
