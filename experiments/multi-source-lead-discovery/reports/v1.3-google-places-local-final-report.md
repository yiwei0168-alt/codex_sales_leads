# v1.3 Google Places Local control and human-calibrated full-pool evaluation

## Executive conclusion

Run `2026-08-27-de-v1.3` supports a clear but deliberately limited conclusion: localized Google Places discovery is the strongest method tested for German local project-service leads, while Exa is the strongest tested source for tier-1 distribution and Tavily is the strongest tested source for B2B resale. Google Places Local has the highest provisional three-lane macro score (`28.03`), ahead of Tavily (`22.13`), but its lead comes almost entirely from project services (`70.3` of the fixed ten-slot channel scale). It is not a universal replacement for web search.

The revised rule set fits the six representative human cases: common-gate agreement, submitted-lane agreement and score-band agreement are all `100%`; quadratic weighted kappa is `1.0`; score MAE is `1.1667`. This is an **in-sample calibration result**, not independent validation, because the same 12 reviewed cases informed the rule changes. No final system winner is declared until a new blind holdout is reviewed.

## Question and frozen scope

The experiment tests discovery quality, not API reliability. Systems receive extra technical attempts when a failure is unrelated to search quality. Evaluation compares candidates only within the same channel, avoiding the invalid assumption that a large distributor is automatically a better discovery result than a small installer.

The frozen market is Germany and the three channels are:

- tier-1 distribution: Distributor or VAD activity with downstream-channel supply;
- B2B resale: Reseller, Dealer, VAR/DVAR, or a mixed B2B online shop demonstrably selling networking goods;
- project services: SI, Installer or MSP activity involving active-networking design, selection, deployment or operation.

A company may have several supported roles. No primary role or business-share estimate is required. A submitted lane passes if public evidence proves any qualifying activity in that lane.

Eight measured systems are present: Gemini Full, product-integrated Gemini discovery, Tavily, SearchAPI, Exa, Brave Search, the original generic Google Places attempt, and the new localized Google Places control.

## Inputs and discovery process

The localized Places control used six short role-intent queries across eight German metro rectangles. Its 24 query/region cells produced 414 raw lane occurrences: 137 tier-1, 135 B2B resale and 142 project services. These aggregated to 360 Places company groups.

Google Places content was treated only as discovery input. It did not satisfy company, role, networking or scoring evidence gates. A separate public-web resolver confirmed 286 official identities; 74 unresolved rows were retained as opaque identifiers and were ineligible for paid fallback. Merging the control with the original experiment produced 465 canonical companies and 555 unique system/lane/company occurrences.

## Shared evidence and scoring

Every occurrence was evaluated from its canonical company's shared provider-neutral dossier. Search snippets, provider summaries and Places descriptions were excluded from scoring. Each company had a cumulative ceiling of five official pages plus two fallback sources.

- 465/465 dossiers completed.
- 1,696 of 1,754 official-page attempts succeeded.
- 98 fallback sources existed cumulatively.
- 28 safely identified, priority-1 dossiers received the paid fallback pass.
- The final evidence states were 33 ready, 331 partial and 101 unsupported.

The six hard gates are company existence, German presence, active-networking relevance, submitted-lane membership, sufficient evidence and canonical uniqueness. Eligible candidates receive:

`product/use-case fit × 9 + cooperation path × 7 + evidence reliability × 4`

Each system/channel contributes at most its best ten eligible candidates. Missing slots score zero. The system macro is the equal mean of the three channel means.

## Human blind audit and initial failure

The user reviewed 12 cases: six representative core cases and six deliberately difficult diagnostic cases, with four cases per lane. System/provider, discovery rank, machine score, occurrence count and core/problem stratum were hidden. All decisions were frozen before deblinding in commit `95df76d`; the decision file SHA-256 is `44f6983305ab8560dd112e53b2d0222596f4fc11e2063eed242e0cc3aac3eafd`.

The pre-revision comparison failed acceptance:

|---|---:|---:|

| Submitted-lane agreement | 0.8333 | ≥ 0.90 |
| Score-band exact agreement | 0.3333 | diagnostic |

The failure was structural, so numeric offsets were prohibited. The disagreement review identified general rule errors rather than provider-specific score errors.

## Executable rule revisions

The following changes were applied to all 465 companies and covered by tests:

1. A generic or software-only `Fachhändler` label no longer proves networking resale.
2. A mixed B2B/B2C online shop qualifies as a Reseller when its own pages prove both B2B commerce and networking-goods sales. E-tailer and Reseller roles are retained together.
3. Repair/consulting pages plus a generic WLAN article no longer prove active-networking commercial involvement without a business action.
4. German VAD signals such as partner portals, `Partner werden`, Systemhaus and Handelspartner supply are recognized as a complete downstream path.
5. VAD identity plus demonstrated active networking establishes moderate product fit; high-end enterprise focus does not collapse the score to generic relevance.
6. Comparable-brand mentions or listings alone no longer receive maximum product fit. Level five requires an explicit partner/distribution path or an active Cudy transaction.
7. Managed operation and deployment count as one execution-control family in project services; they do not independently prove selection or procurement influence.
8. Guest-Wi-Fi specialists in hospitality/SMB settings, relevant product families, enterprise-only use cases and auditable live commerce received explicit deterministic treatment.

The calibrated full-pool run produced 51 eligible and 504 rejected occurrences, compared with 47/508 before revision.

## Interpretation

Google Places Local is valuable because geographically tiled, short-intent queries reach small local installers whose websites and SEO footprint are weak. Its 12 eligible project-service occurrences fill the ten-slot target and make it the best tested local-service discovery complement. Its one eligible tier-1 and one eligible B2B result show that it is a poor standalone source for channel-structure roles.

For tier-1 distribution, Exa has the highest channel result (`26.5`). For B2B resale, Tavily has the highest result (`16.7`). Tavily and SearchAPI are the strongest balanced web-discovery sources in this run. Product Gemini improved after mixed B2B shops were recognized, but neither Gemini mode produced competitive three-lane coverage under the provider-neutral public-proof gates.

The recommended product architecture is therefore a routed ensemble: localized Places for Installer/SI discovery; Exa and web search for tier-1/VAD discovery; Tavily/SearchAPI for B2B shops and service evidence; then shared official-site enrichment, canonical deduplication and deterministic role/path scoring.

## Limitations and next acceptance step

- Only Germany and three channels were measured.
- The representative human sample contains six cases; six additional cases are diagnostic, not acceptance statistics.
- The rule revision learned from these same 12 decisions, so the perfect categorical fit is expected to be optimistic.
- Public evidence absence means “not demonstrated,” not “business does not exist.” This particularly affects small companies and opaque Places rows.
- The provisional macro is influenced by the fixed ten-slot policy and should be read alongside each channel result.

The next stage is a fresh blind holdout selected after v1.3.1 was frozen. It must contain no training cases and should oversample B2B mixed-role shops, enterprise VADs, local installers and repair/consulting near-misses. Until that holdout passes the same thresholds, the leaderboard status remains `post-rule-revision-independent-validation-pending` and no final winner is declared.
