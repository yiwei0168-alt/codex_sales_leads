# v1.3 Google Places Local control, full-pool rescoring and calibration checkpoint

## Status

Run `2026-08-27-de-v1.3` has completed discovery, independent identity resolution, shared evidence collection, all-candidate rule scoring and blind-audit sampling. The scores in this document are **pre-human-calibration** and cannot support a final winner until the frozen 12-case audit is completed.

## Google Places Local control

The added system is `product-google-places-local`. It uses six short role-intent queries across eight German metro rectangles. Places submission does not require a cited webpage. Hard discovery filters require `OPERATIONAL` status and a German postal address/country code; API place types are metadata, not role evidence.

The 24 query/region cells produced 414 lane occurrences: 137 tier-1, 135 B2B resale and 142 project-service occurrences before company aggregation. Aggregation produced 360 Places company groups. An independent official-page pass resolved 286 groups; the remaining 74 retain only an opaque place-ID-derived identifier. Their Places names, addresses, coordinates and descriptive content are not committed as scoring evidence.

After canonical domain merging with the original experiment, the shared pool contains 465 companies and 555 system/lane occurrences. The Local control contributes 125 unique canonical candidates in tier-1, 125 in B2B resale and 142 in project services.

## Shared evidence

Every company uses the same provider-neutral evidence policy and cumulative budget: up to five official pages plus two fallback sources. Discovery snippets, provider summaries and Places content are excluded from scoring.

- 465/465 dossiers completed.
- 1,696 of 1,754 attempted official pages were collected.
- 98 fallback sources exist cumulatively, including inherited evidence from the original pool.
- The paid priority-1 pass covered only 28 safely identifiable dossiers. Opaque Places rows and candidates with exhausted budgets were excluded.
- Final dossier status: 33 ready across every requested lane, 331 partially supported, 101 still needing enrichment, zero identity conflicts.
- The per-occurrence scorer does not require every requested lane for the same company to pass; it tests the submitted lane independently, consistent with the confirmed multi-role rule.

## Full-pool scoring

All 555 unique system/lane/company occurrences were scored. Forty-seven pass all six gates; 508 fail at least one gate. The gates are company identity, German presence, active-networking relevance, submitted-lane business, sufficient evidence and canonical uniqueness.

Scores use the frozen weights: product/use-case fit ×9, cooperation path ×7 and evidence reliability ×4, maximum 100. Each system/channel ranks at most its best ten eligible companies. A missing target slot contributes zero; channels are compared only with the same channel, and the system macro is the equal mean of the three ten-slot channel means.

These values expose two hypotheses rather than a conclusion. First, localized Places queries are much better at discovering installers and local service companies than distributor/reseller business models. Second, the executable submitted-lane recognizer rejects many candidates, including previously human-accepted companies, so its category calibration must be audited before interpreting the large rank changes. In the Local control alone, submitted-lane membership fails 357 occurrences and active-networking evidence fails 307; this is evidence about the current public-proof/rule boundary, not proof that those businesses do not perform the activity.

## Frozen human calibration

The new packet contains 12 companies: six core cases and six diagnostic problem cases, with four cases per lane. Internally, each lane includes Local-control and non-control cases where the eligible pool allows it. The packet hides system/provider, discovery rank, rule score, occurrence count and core/problem stratum. It retains the review lane, company identity, official URL and the same provider-neutral evidence available to the scorer.

The reviewer records all supported categories; no primary role is required. Calibration remains a reference check: any numeric adjustment must be one uniform category-level offset applied to every system and capped at ±8 points. No deblinding or correction occurs before all 12 decisions are frozen.
