# CO-retail v2.0.5 localization and primary-role incident

v2.0.5 restored the semantic pipeline. From 67 cached inputs, correction returned 66 outputs, 57 model-valid roles and 15 Retailer/E-tailer primary-role candidates; qualification returned 15 outputs, 12 completed assessments and 6 eligible final candidates. Correction used 247,132 input tokens, 126,475 output tokens, zero reasoning tokens and USD 0.0630092. Qualification used 111,601 input tokens, 22,879 output tokens, zero reasoning tokens and USD 0.0345714. Cumulative experiment cost became USD 2.1411647; forecast was USD 16.5645 expected and USD 19.7119 upper. No USD 10 checkpoint was crossed.

The remaining underfill was caused upstream. Every Spanish-language market shared Mexico's hard-coded round focuses, so Colombia queries contained Mexico City, Guadalajara, Monterrey, Puebla, Querétaro, Tijuana, León and Mérida. The cached pool consequently included companies from Mexico, Peru, Spain, Guatemala, El Salvador, Costa Rica, Panama and Venezuela. Exa `exa.ai/library/organization/...` profile URLs were accepted as company domains, causing unrelated profiles to merge. Incomplete Latin American public-suffix handling produced identities such as `com.pe`. Nineteen candidates had a Hybrid primary role; several, including Alkosto, supported only Retailer and E-tailer within one family and should have had a concrete retail primary role.

v2.0.6 keys city focuses by country code, blocks Exa-owned profile URLs from candidate identity, adds the observed Latin American public suffixes, and permits Hybrid only across multiple co-primary families. The repair-search path retains cached valid candidates and evidence, deterministically normalizes same-family Hybrid, retries only incomplete cached scores, excludes cached domains, and then performs up to five corrected Colombia acquisition rounds. No previous spend or failure is removed.
