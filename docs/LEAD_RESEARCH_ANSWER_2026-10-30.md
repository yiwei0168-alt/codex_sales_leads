# Lead review supplementary answers

Rule `LEAD-RESEARCH-ANSWER-01`, confirmed 2026-10-30: accept an owner's supplementary answer for the corresponding account, task, company and original review question; retain its source/time; resume the affected review from its saved checkpoint; preserve completed discovery and scoring; show receipt, continued review and unresolved evidence accurately. Confirmation does not establish implementation, quality acceptance or cooperation interest.

Implementation: an authenticated `GET/POST /api/assistant/actions/[id]/research-answer` provides the original pending question and an immutable owner answer. Ownership comes only from the session. An action-row lock serializes answer submission with normal confirmation; the answer and worker queue transition commit together. Repeated identical submissions return the original receipt without another enqueue; conflicting submissions cannot overwrite history. Stale questions and running jobs are rejected.

Automatic local resolution is deliberately limited to the exact fully scoped Cudy current-relationship question. Only an explicit no-current-relationship selection can resolve its gate. Existing/unknown answers remain pending. Other unknown gates, contradictory relationship judgments, conflicting correction findings, broader questions, and normalization failures remain unresolved. The existing judge's provisional dimensions are normalized through the unchanged rubric; the primary assessment and original judge response remain historical evidence. Completion is labeled `owner-evidence-resolved`, with a dated receipt, rather than claiming a new blind model judgment. The declaration does not prove commercial interest.

Private owner statements are kept in `lead_research_answer`, scoped by user/action/thread/company/question hash. They are never added to public evidence or external secondary/judge payloads. App-role RLS and SELECT/INSERT-only privileges protect isolation and history. Migration 173 is additive. A future change to a received declaration requires a separately designed revision/review flow; this version refuses replacement.

The existing failed search-plan card now displays supported supplementary questions and submission choices. It reports receipt and queued continuation without claiming acceptance. Other question types remain visibly unresolved. The original conversation and results entry stay in place.

Verification: 66 focused tests passed, including authentication, strict input, stale/foreign binding, identical retry, non-resolution cases, cached review reuse, and graph recovery without another scoring/discovery call. TypeScript, production build and lint passed (two existing unused-variable warnings). Real database rollback probes verified owner insertion, peer read denial, peer action insertion denial and update denial. An offline replay of the actual saved task reused one secondary and one judge response with zero network requests and no persisted acceptance decision.

Deployment and actual task completion are tracked separately; the offline result alone does not establish final delivery or business acceptance. Broader free-form research, declaration revision and the remaining channel cases are not represented as completed by this change.
