# Stage 214: provider-returned versus admitted discovery volume

The discovery adapter result now carries optional aggregate `providerReturnedItems` and `providerOverdeliveredItems`. Google Places, Exa, Brave and SearchAPI count the respective provider result rows; Gemini counts extracted public URLs. Each adapter still passes at most the requested item count into candidate processing. The hybrid executor's `rawResults` now uses the actual provider row/link count when present, while `items` and `normalizedCompanies` remain admitted/normalized counts. It records `provider-overdelivery` and `provider-unusable-row` separately in discarded reasons; `rejectedResults` includes both plus registry exclusions. An older cached result without these fields falls back to its admitted item count rather than inventing historical raw volume.

Three mocked over-delivery responses of five rows each for requests of three reported 15 provider rows, nine admitted items and six over-delivered rows. A separate synthetic hybrid call reported two over-delivered and one unusable row alongside its accepted/normalized counts. Provider adapters and hybrid telemetry tests, full **1,039 tests in 208 files**, TypeScript, focused ESLint and production build passed. No route, target, scoring rule, provider price or final-qualified count changed.

This is aggregate internal telemetry only. It does not assert that provider links are unique companies, that every admitted item is valid, or that accepted result slots are final qualified leads. The live A11 whole-run cost bound remains absent. No customer task, paid search/model call, credit, token, cash or mail was used.

Efficiency: four synthetic telemetry inputs (three adapter over-deliveries and one hybrid aggregation), with 15 known raw rows and nine admitted rows in the adapter cases; six excess rows and the hybrid case's one unusable row were discarded for stated reasons. Local tests used all valid mock observations, product downstream use and user adoption remain unknown. Three adapter mock transports, zero paid credits/cash/tokens/external retries; full regression about 20 seconds, real provider latency unknown.
